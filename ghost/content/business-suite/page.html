Most growing businesses run on a patchwork: one tool for leads, another for invoices, a spreadsheet for inventory, a separate HR system and a shared inbox for support. Frequently Asked Questions

What software is it built on?

The suite uses ERPNext, Frappe CRM, Frappe HR and Frappe Helpdesk, all open-source applications on the Frappe platform, plus Zenphry's executive and governance additions.

Can we move from QuickBooks or Zoho Books?

Yes. We bring over your opening balances and current-year transactions and check the totals against your old system. Your old system stays the official record until those checks are signed off.

Can we start with one module?

Yes. Many teams start with CRM and accounting, then add purchasing, projects, HR or helpdesk when they are ready. Everything is already connected, so each addition builds on the same records.

Does it calculate and file US payroll taxes?

The suite holds payroll records and salary slips. US payroll tax calculation and filing usually stay with your payroll provider or accountant. We confirm the right setup during discovery.

Who owns our data?

You do. The platform is open source, and your data can be exported in standard formats at any time.

Can we see it before deciding?

Yes. Book a call and we will walk you through a live demo on a sample company covering CRM, sales, purchasing, inventory, projects, HR, helpdesk and governance.
